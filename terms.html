CombinedTution Terms of Service

Please read these terms carefully before engaging with our platform. They define our service agreements, tutor verification policies, and guardian satisfaction guarantees.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Governing Law: Bangladesh

1. Platform Overview & Eligibility

CombinedTution serves as a verified educational matching marketplace in Bangladesh. By registering an account or contacting a tutor through our platform, you agree to these Terms. Users must be at least 18 years old or under guardian supervision when submitting tuition requests.

2. Tutor Verification & Accountability

All educators listed on CombinedTution must undergo mandatory 4-step credential verification:

  • Submission of Government National ID (NID) and permanent residence verification.
  • Verification of current university student ID or graduation certificate (BUET, DU, Medical Colleges, etc.).
  • Academic transcript verification for SSC, HSC, or O/A Level background.
  • Any misrepresentation of academic credentials results in immediate termination of the tutor's account.

3. Trial Demo Session & Guardian Approval

Guardians are entitled to conduct a trial demo class with shortlisted tutors to observe teaching methodologies and rapport with the student. Guardians are under no obligation to continue if the teaching style does not match their requirements.

4. Free Tutor Replacement Policy

If at any point during the tuition arrangement a guardian is dissatisfied with academic results, punctuality, or attendance, CombinedTution's academic coordinators will assign a qualified replacement tutor free of additional matching charges.

5. Tuition Fees & Payment Terms

Monthly honorariums and fees are agreed upon mutually between the guardian and educator based on class grade, subjects, and weekly schedules. CombinedTution charges zero hidden platform fees to guardians.
