Everything you need to know about the CombinedTution hiring and teaching process.

Our tutor search and matching service is 100% free for guardians and students. You only pay the agreed monthly tuition fee directly to the assigned tutor after your classes commence.

Every tutor must upload their National ID card (NID), valid university student ID (e.g. BUET, DU, DMC, NSU, BRAC), and educational certificates. Our verification team reviews and approves these before granting a verified badge.

You are under no obligation to continue. We provide a completely free tutor replacement, sending you alternative verified candidates within 24 hours until you are completely satisfied.
